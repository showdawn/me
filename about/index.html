About me.

I'm Sachidananda Singh, and most people call me Sachin. I'm an engineering leader working where distributed systems meet supply chain operations.

I set out to work on computers, and the supply chain kept happening to me. At AWS I worked on keeping millions of servers in agreement about the time. At Wayfair I met the same problems in delivery. At Hims & Hers Health, where I lead supply chain and fulfillment engineering today, I met them again in inventory and receiving.

Somewhere in those years I realized I'd been working on the same system all along. Sometimes it was made of servers and sometimes it was made of trucks. I wrote Supply Chains Are Distributed Systems to write that translation down.

I also review and judge other people's work. I'm an IEEE Senior Member, and I live in Austin, Texas.
