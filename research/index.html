Research and review

Contributing to research and the profession.

I publish research, review other people's work and judge engineering programs. My areas are distributed systems, supply chain software and applied AI.

Published research

My research looks at how data and prediction improve supply chain decisions. In 2026 the journal Acta Innovations published a paper I wrote with Mohammed Muqthar Ghori on predictive analytics and real time data in supply chain optimization. It examines how historical transaction data can give earlier warning of supply chain risk.

The longer argument is in my book, which maps distributed systems ideas such as consistency, idempotency and reconciliation onto physical supply chains.

  • Intelligent Supply Chain Optimization Using Predictive Analytics and Real-Time DataActa Innovations, volume 63, number 1s, 2026
  • Supply Chains Are Distributed SystemsBook, 2026

Artifact evaluation

Artifact evaluation tests whether the code and data behind a research paper work the way the paper says. Committee members build the software, run the experiments and report what they find.

I served on the Artifact Evaluation Committees for ACM SIGCOMM 2026, the annual flagship conference of ACM's data communication group, and for ACM CCS 2026, the ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security.
