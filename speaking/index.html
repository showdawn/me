Speaking

Talks on software that meets the physical world.

Sessions for engineering and operations audiences on inventory consistency and distributed systems in logistics.

Topics

30 minutes · Backend engineers and engineering leaders

When retries change the inventory count

A warehouse event can arrive twice, arrive late or disappear from the consumer's view. This session uses a small synthetic inventory stream to show the difference between delivery guarantees and correct state.

Attendees leave able to explain where duplicate suppression belongs and to design a recovery test around a watermark.

30 minutes · Engineering managers, architects and operations leaders

The inventory promise across a software boundary

An available quantity is a promise made using information from several systems. This session follows a fictional order across a storefront, a warehouse and a carrier, and examines which confirmation makes a promise safe.

Attendees leave with questions they can apply in an architecture review.
