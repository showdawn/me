The book

Supply Chains Are Distributed Systems

How Engineering Thinking and AI Are Rebuilding the Way the World Moves Goods

By Sachidananda Singh

A book for engineering leaders working with physical operations, and operations leaders working with software. It examines inventory consistency, message passing, reconciliation and the role of AI in operational decisions.

View on AmazonAuthor page

What the book explores

State

Inventory and agreement

How different systems represent physical inventory, and what happens when those records disagree.

Communication

Events and message passing

How information travels between organizations and software, and how failures propagate.

Reliability

Recovery and reconciliation

How engineering patterns can help operations detect discrepancies and recover from failures.

Applied AI

Decisions and their foundations

Why reliable inputs and operational controls matter when AI participates in supply chain decisions.
