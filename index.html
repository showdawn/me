Hello, I'm Sachidananda Singh.

Engineering leader, author of Supply Chains Are Distributed Systems and IEEE Senior Member. Most people call me Sachin.

Briefly about me.

  • I lead supply chain and fulfillment engineering at Hims & Hers Health, and I live in Austin, Texas.
  • Before that I led the teams behind last mile delivery and fleet software at Wayfair. Earlier I was a software engineer at AWS, working on Amazon Time Sync Service.
  • My book Supply Chains Are Distributed Systems came out in 2026. It's for engineering and operations leaders who have to keep software records and physical goods in agreement.
  • I review other people's work. I've served on Artifact Evaluation Committees for ACM SIGCOMM and ACM CCS, I review manuscripts for the journal Informatica, and I judged the CES Innovation Awards.
  • I'm an IEEE Senior Member and a member of ACM. My paper on predictive analytics in supply chains appeared in Acta Innovations in 2026.
